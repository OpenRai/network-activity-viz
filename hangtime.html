How to read this view

Marks show the last 60 seconds of confirmed blocks. Horizontal position is observer receipt time; vertical position is amount on a log scale. Receives remain visible whether or not their source sends were observed. This chart does not measure settlement latency.

The consensus layer

Bars and counts measure vote messages, not delegated voting weight. The endpoint reports each block’s confirmation; quorum is not inferred.
